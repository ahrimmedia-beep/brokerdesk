# BrokerDesk

AI platform for real estate agencies. It answers every new lead in seconds by voice or chat, qualifies the client and sends a ready lead to the agent's CRM.

## What it does

- Talks to clients by voice right in the browser, and by chat on the website and in WhatsApp.
- Asks the questions an agent would ask: budget, area, timing, cash or mortgage.
- Creates the lead in the CRM with a short summary of the conversation.
- Works in English and Russian.

## Stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, OpenAI Realtime API (WebRTC voice), Redis, HubSpot API, Meta Conversions API, Vercel.
